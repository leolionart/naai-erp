# ERP-881 summary

Owner Current now presents two separate populations:

- a confirmed cash timeline containing owner-paid company costs, owner funding and company
  bank/cash repayments to the owner;
- a review list containing invoice-only, company-funded or otherwise unproven Owner Current entries.

The confirmed timeline calculates the balance immediately after each confirmed movement in
chronological order. The complete mapped ledger closing balance remains separately disclosed and
continues to feed the unchanged executive dashboard metric.
