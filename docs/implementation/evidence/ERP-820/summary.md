# ERP-820 Summary

Added a focused owner-paid small-business ingestion contract. The default model assumes one owner
pays most company costs from a personal account: supplier/payee identity remains canonical, project
allocation remains unchanged, and the company credits a reviewed Owner Payable/current-account
liability instead of pretending company bank/cash paid.

The guide prohibits importing the owner's full personal statement and documents the missing
first-class owner reimbursement/custody workflow. Advanced petty-cash and accountable-advance
operations are explicitly outside the default model.
